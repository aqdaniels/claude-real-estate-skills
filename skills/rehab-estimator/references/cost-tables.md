# Cost tables, regional multipliers, and deal math

These are national mid-market baselines (installed, labor + materials, licensed trades, non-union, 2025–2026 pricing) at multiplier 1.00. They are for screening, not bidding. Always multiply by the regional factor, run one or two calibration searches for the big-ticket items (roof, HVAC, foundation) in the subject's metro, and state in the report that contractor bids replace these numbers before an offer is finalized.

## Scenario definitions

- **Rent-ready**: everything safe and functional; repair over replace; rental-grade finishes (LVP, stock cabinets, laminate, basic package); paint; landscaping cleanup. Defers B-grade systems. Goal is durable and low-maintenance, not attractive.
- **Mid-grade**: what renovated sold comps in this ZIP show. Search `<zip> renovated sold` and look at three comps' descriptions; match, don't exceed. Typically new kitchen and baths at mid spec, all flooring, all paint, fixtures, lighting, curb appeal; systems replaced if C or worse.
- **Full / worst case**: mid-grade plus every hidden risk materialising at its cost plus every D-grade and U-grade system replaced. This is the number to hold in reserve, not the expected cost.

Report all three even when the user asked for one; the spread between rent-ready and full is itself the risk metric.

## Deal impact

Show a three-column table (low / mid / high rehab) for the strategy in play.

**BRRRR** — Max purchase = 0.75 × ARV − rehab (use rent-ready or mid-grade per the user's rent target) − holding − closing. Refi at 75% LTV of ARV; cash left in deal = purchase + rehab + holding + closing − loan. Report cash left in and, with rent, the post-refi cash flow and DSCR.

**Flip** — Max purchase = 0.70 × ARV − rehab (mid-grade) − holding − selling costs. Margin = ARV − all-in. Show margin % of ARV; flag if < 10%.

**Buy-and-hold** — All-in basis = price + rehab (rent-ready). Recompute $/sqft vs comps, cash needed, and cash-on-cash with rehab in the denominator. List deferred capex from B-grade items with expected timing (roof at year N, HVAC at year N) so the reserve is sized honestly.

**Owner-occupant** — Budget = mid-grade + contingency. Separate "before move-in" (safety, systems) from "can wait". List the C/D items worth requesting as seller credits, with the cost range to cite.

**ARV source** — from property-analysis if run; otherwise 3–5 renovated sold comps in the ZIP within 6 months, ±20% sqft, adjusted $/sqft × subject sqft; state a confidence level (High ≥4 tight comps / Moderate 3 / Low ≤2 or wide radius) exactly as property-analysis would.
